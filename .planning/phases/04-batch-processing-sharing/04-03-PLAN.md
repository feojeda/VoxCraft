    async def serve_shared_wav(token: str) -> FileResponse:

        share = await share_service.get_share_by_token(token)

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-04-09 | Spoofing | Share token | mitigate | `secrets.token_urlsafe(32)` produces 256-bit entropy; guessing probability is cryptographically negligible |
| T-04-10 | Information Disclosure | Public audio | mitigate | Token validated against database on every request; expired/revoked tokens return 404; only completed job audio is served |
| T-04-11 | Elevation of Privilege | Share revocation | mitigate | `revoke_share` verifies `share.user_id == user_id` before updating; users cannot revoke others' shares |
| T-04-12 | Denial of Service | Share creation | mitigate | Only completed jobs can be shared; prevents sharing of incomplete/failed jobs that might be re-created in loops |
| T-04-13 | Information Disclosure | Job enumeration | accept | Token is 43 chars of base64url; no sequential IDs exposed; low risk of enumeration |
</threat_model>
